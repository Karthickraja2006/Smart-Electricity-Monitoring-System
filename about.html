Why we built this

Most of us only think about electricity when the bill lands. SmartElectricity gives you a small daily habit that makes the bill predictable.

How it works

First, log the units you used each day. Then check your dashboard for today's cost and your month so far. Finally, look at your appliance list to see what is using the most, and change one habit at a time.

How we work out your numbers

Your cost is the units you used multiplied by ₹8 a unit. That's an estimate to help you plan, so your real bill may differ a little. We treat 12 units in a day as a comfortable limit for a home, and we'll tell you when you pass it.
